## `senior_doctor.png`

```
Official "Hobo Evolution" game asset. 2D flat vector cartoon, single art direction. Big-head/tiny-body (head 55-60% of figure). Uniform thick black outline, constant weight. Flat colors, minimal cel-shading, NO gradients, single soft light top-left. Palette: #FFD93D #FF6B35 #FF4D6D #4D96FF #6BCB77 with #FFF8E7 / #2C2C2C. Full-body standing character, both feet planted flat on the ground, hands visible, characterful idle pose, centered, generous safe margin, square canvas, plain white background. Clean readable silhouette, adult-comedy tone, humorous expression. Mobile-game production quality, cohesive studio look. Subject: veteran senior doctor standing perfectly upright, one hand adjusting the head mirror on his forehead, a framed diploma tucked under the other arm. Props: cool grey-blue clinical coat (never pure white), head mirror, framed diploma under arm, expensive pen in chest pocket, gold wristwatch. Cultural cues: renowned specialist with a private practice. Wealth cues: wealthy professional, gold wristwatch. Expression: calm reassuring smile. His coat is the whole problem with this asset: draw it in a cool grey-blue off-white around #D2DCE8, with clear blue-grey folds and shadows around #9FB0C4 and a darker seam line, so it never approaches the white of the paper. Same for the diploma sheet and the head mirror.

CUTOUT RULES — this asset gets its white background removed automatically by the pipeline, so these two are hard requirements, above any styling choice:
(1) NOTHING on the figure may be white or near-white. Pure white belongs to the background alone. Every fold, every prop and every highlight must read clearly darker or more saturated than the paper behind it — if any region would come out lighter than #BFE0F5, deepen it until it does not.
(2) The background must never be trapped inside the drawing. No frame, card, sticker panel, banner, backdrop, scenery, ground platform, ground line or cast shadow. No closed loop or ring of any kind: a rope, lasso or strap must be drawn open, its ends trailing out of frame or overlapping the body, never closing a circle in mid-air. No hand on hip — every gap between an arm and the torso stays open to the outside, or the arm touches the body. Nothing detached from the figure: no floating debris and no free-standing sparkles; any glint must touch the silhouette.

Full body standing pose, feet planted on the ground, hands visible, centered, plain white background.
```
