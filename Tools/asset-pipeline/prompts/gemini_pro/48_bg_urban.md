## Prompt

Picture a lively Buenos Aires street corner in the middle of a sunny, busy day. A corner kiosk stall with snack strips hanging off it sits on one side, a bus-stop shelter on the other, and a tangle of power lines crisscrosses overhead with a few pigeons perched on them. Faded posters cling to the walls, the shutters look a little worn, and in the distance, silhouetted against the bright noon sky, stands the Obelisco. This is downtown Buenos Aires energy — a kiosk on every corner.

The bottom third of the image must be a clean, empty, walkable gray sidewalk where game characters will stand — keep that whole strip completely free of objects, props or clutter. All scenery, structures and landscape belong in the upper two thirds. Do not include any characters, people, or text of any kind. Flat cartoon style with thick black outlines and flat colors matching a vibrant, colorful mobile game — no photorealism, no gradients, no watermark. Square image, full canvas, no cropping.
