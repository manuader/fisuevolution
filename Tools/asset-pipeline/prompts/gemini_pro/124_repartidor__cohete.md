> ⚠️ **Regeneración.** La primera pasada salió un personaje distinto —un vendedor
> callejero con bandeja de golosinas y un gato a los pies—, sin jetpack, sin
> antiparras y sin ninguna relación con el cadete de la referencia.
>
> El prompt viejo tenía dos fallas que lo explican:
>
> 1. **Le sacaba los dos anclajes de silueta a la vez.** El Repartidor ES la
>    mochila-cubo gigante más el casco; el prompt pedía "la bici ya no está" y
>    reemplazaba la mochila por una cápsula, o sea que borraba lo único que lo
>    hacía reconocible y dejaba al modelo sin nada a lo que agarrarse.
> 2. **Pedía una paleta que NO contrasta.** Decía "boldly different in color" y
>    después pedía *hot orange* — y la mochila del personaje base ya es naranja.
>
> Esta versión **reskinea los anclajes en vez de borrarlos** (la mochila-cubo pasa
> a ser un módulo de carga orbital del mismo volumen; el casco, uno espacial con
> la visera levantada) y se va a azul profundo + blanco + cian, sin naranja.

## Prompt

Match EXACTLY the art style, line weight, proportions and color treatment of the attached reference character — same game, same studio.

This is THE SAME CHARACTER as the attached reference: the food delivery courier from Fisura Evolution, an Argentine merge-idle mobile game. It is an ALTERNATE OUTFIT, not a new character. Keep his face, his beard, his stressed wide-eyed hurry, his slightly bent sprinting knees, one hand gripping a phone and the other thumb hooked in the strap. Keep his SILHOUETTE: a helmet on his head and a huge cube-shaped cargo box towering over his back — those two shapes are what make him recognizable and they must stay.

"Rocket Courier" outfit — reskin those same shapes into orbital delivery: the cube cargo box becomes a white armored orbital cargo module of the SAME size and position, with a glowing cyan seal and two small thrusters at its lower corners venting a short blue flame; the helmet becomes a rounded white space helmet with the glass visor flipped UP over his forehead; his jacket becomes a padded deep-blue flight suit with reflective cyan piping; his worn sneakers become chunky white magnetic boots. He still holds his cracked phone.

Palette: deep navy blue, clean white and glowing cyan. NO orange anywhere — the reference already uses orange for the box, and this outfit has to read as a completely different one at a glance.

Do NOT turn him into a different character: no market stall, no vendor tray, no goods to sell, no animals, no bicycle. Same person, same pose, new gear.

Full body standing character, both feet planted on the ground, hands visible, complete figure with generous margins, centered on a plain pure white background, square image, no text, no watermark, no cropping.
