## Prompt

Picture a gritty, dead-end alley in Buenos Aires at night — this is where El Fisura sleeps. The walls are cracked plaster covered in graffiti, tall stacks of flattened cardboard lean against the back wall, and a battered dumpster sits tucked to one side. A clothesline strung with rags hangs overhead, a pipe drips steadily, and a single lonely street lamp casts a soft, warm glow from the top-left, glinting off a narrow strip of night sky visible between the rooftops. The mood is moody but warm — gritty, not grim.

The bottom third of the image must be a clean, empty, walkable stretch of worn cobblestone where game characters will stand — keep that whole strip completely free of objects, props or clutter. All scenery, structures and landscape belong in the upper two thirds. Do not include any characters, people, or text of any kind. Flat cartoon style with thick black outlines and flat colors matching a vibrant, colorful mobile game — no photorealism, no gradients, no watermark. Square image, full canvas, no cropping.
