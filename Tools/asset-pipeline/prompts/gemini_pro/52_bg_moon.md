## Prompt

Picture the surface of the Moon, now private real estate. Cratered gray hills roll through the midground, several flags have been planted at different points in the distance, and a small glass dome habitat sits on the horizon. Earth rises big, blue and glowing against a black, star-filled sky. The mood is serene cosmic quiet, lit by cool blue earthlight.

The bottom third of the image must be a clean, empty, walkable stretch of smooth gray moon dust where game characters will stand — keep that whole strip completely free of objects, props or clutter. All scenery, structures and landscape belong in the upper two thirds. Do not include any characters, people, or text of any kind. Flat cartoon style with thick black outlines and flat colors matching a vibrant, colorful mobile game — no photorealism, no gradients, no watermark. Square image, full canvas, no cropping.
