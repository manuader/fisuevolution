## Prompt

Match EXACTLY the art style, outline weight and color treatment of the attached reference icon - same game, same studio. The attached image is ONLY a style guide: do NOT draw a trophy cup, draw only the new subject described below.

Ranking is the icon of the RANKING card in the menu of Fisura Evolution, an Argentine merge-idle mobile game. Design it as a cartoon WINNERS PODIUM, front-facing and symmetrical: three rectangular blocks side by side, the tall middle block in sunny yellow #FFD93D, the left block of medium height in blue #4D96FF, the right block the shortest in orange #FF6B35. Each block has a thick ink #2C2C2C outline, a cream #FFF8E7 highlight strip along its top edge and a couple of drawn horizontal seams, with a bold five-point star emblem on the front of the middle block in pink-red #FF4D6D. On top of the middle block sits a big round gold medal with a ribbon: a sunny yellow #FFD93D disc with an ink outline and a pink-red #FF4D6D star in the center, hanging from a blue #4D96FF V-shaped ribbon that lies over the podium. One white #FFFFFF four-point sparkle glints at the upper-left of the medal.

This icon is drawn at 60pt on a menu card, so a little internal detail is welcome, but every stroke stays thick and the whole idea has to land in one glance. The background removal step of our pipeline eats large flat interior areas that look like background, so every enclosed shape must be a closed silhouette filled with a saturated palette color and carrying drawn internal detail instead of one empty plane, and nothing inside the icon may be pure white except the sparkle.

Official game asset, same visual language as the rest of the game: 2D flat vector cartoon, single art direction, uniform thick black outline of constant weight, flat colors with minimal cel-shading, NO gradients, no photorealism, single soft light from the top-left. Locked palette - #FFD93D sunny yellow, #FF6B35 orange, #FF4D6D pink-red, #4D96FF blue, #6BCB77 green, plus #FFF8E7 cream, #2C2C2C ink black and #FFFFFF white - use no colors outside that list. Generous safe margin, square canvas, clean readable shapes, mobile-game production quality, cohesive studio look. Simple flat vector game icon, centered, plain white background. No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no cropping.
