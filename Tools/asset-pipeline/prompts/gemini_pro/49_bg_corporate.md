## Prompt

Picture a glass-and-steel corporate financial district in the cool light of morning. Mirrored office towers with grids of lit windows rise on both sides, their revolving-door entrances tucked at the far edges, and a small coffee cart sits off to one side. A thin strip of pale morning sky is visible between the skyscrapers. The mood is polished and ambitious — all blue glass and steel.

The bottom third of the image must be a clean, empty, walkable polished granite plaza where game characters will stand — keep that whole strip completely free of objects, props or clutter. All scenery, structures and landscape belong in the upper two thirds. Do not include any characters, people, or text of any kind. Flat cartoon style with thick black outlines and flat colors matching a vibrant, colorful mobile game — no photorealism, no gradients, no watermark. Square image, full canvas, no cropping.
