## Prompt

Match EXACTLY the art style, line weight, proportions and color treatment of the attached reference character — same game, same studio.

⚠️ STYLE IS THE POINT OF THIS REGENERATION. The previous attempt was rejected for breaking the game's look, so these are hard requirements, not suggestions: **thick, even black ink outlines** around the head, hair, collar and every shape — the same weight as the attached reference. **Flat cel shading only**: solid blocks of color with at most one darker flat tone for shadow. NO airbrushing, NO soft gradients on the skin or hair, NO painterly rendering, NO 3D shading, NO glossy highlights. And it is a **CARICATURE, not a portrait**: exaggerate the features well past realism — big rubbery mouth, oversized expressive eyes, comic proportions. A handsome, realistically-proportioned face is WRONG for this game.

FACE CLOSE-UP of El Trapito, the street parking minder who charges you for watching your own car, from Fisura Evolution, an Argentine merge-idle mobile game where a street survivor evolves all the way into a god. Same character as the attached reference, head and shoulders only, filling the frame. Keep only the head-level identifiers: oversized fluorescent vest collar swallowing his neck, plastic whistle on a string, the filthy orange rag draped over one shoulder. His face is doing its signature gag: an insistent friendly-but-not-really grin, eyebrows up, already talking you into paying. Push the expression further than in the full-body art — this crop is shown small at the left edge of a card in the upgrades menu, so the joke has to read at thumbnail size.

Head and shoulders only, face centered and filling most of the frame, no hands, nothing below the chest, square image, no text, no watermark, do not crop the top of the head. The background must be **pure flat white, completely empty, edge to edge and into all four corners** — the image gets cut out automatically, so any colored backdrop bleeding behind the shoulders ruins it. Nothing but the character floats on white.
