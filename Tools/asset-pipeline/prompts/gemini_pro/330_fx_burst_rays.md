## Prompt

FX Burst Rays is a background effect sprite for Fisura Evolution, an Argentine merge-idle mobile game: a simple twelve-ray pinwheel that spins slowly behind a treasure chest. CRITICAL CONSTRAINT, more important than anything else in this prompt: the area BETWEEN the rays is EMPTY PLAIN WHITE BACKGROUND. Do NOT fill the gaps with black, with dark grey, or with any color. Do not draw a dark disc, a dark square or a dark backdrop anywhere. The finished image must be twelve pale shapes floating on white and nothing else. Draw EXACTLY TWELVE straight-sided triangular rays radiating from a single center point, evenly spaced at thirty degrees. Every ray is identical: filled flat with cream #FFF8E7 and carrying a uniform thick ink #2C2C2C outline along both long sides and across its blunt outer tip. Every ray is FAT -- at its outer end each ray is about as wide as the white gap next to it, so rays and gaps split the circle roughly half and half. Twelve rays only: not twenty, not thirty, and none of them thin or needle-like. At the very center sits one small solid ink #2C2C2C disc, about one fifteenth of the canvas wide, that all twelve rays spring from -- it is the only dark filled area in the whole image. UNLIKE our other assets this one must REACH THE EDGES of the canvas: the ray tips run all the way out to the four sides, with no safe margin. IMPORTANT: the rays must stay cream -- the game tints this sprite at runtime to four different rarity colors by multiplying, so any ray filled black or dark would stay black no matter what color is applied, and the effect would be dead. Nothing else in the image: no chest, no stars, no clouds, no circle around the rays.

This art is used at two very different sizes -- huge in a full-screen opening animation and at 26pt inside a list row -- so it has to survive at thumbnail size: one dominant silhouette, thick strokes, strong internal contrast, and no element smaller than roughly one twelfth of the canvas.

The background removal step of our pipeline eats large flat interior areas that look like background, so every enclosed shape must be a closed silhouette filled with a saturated palette color and carrying drawn internal detail -- planks, grain, rivets or highlights -- instead of one empty plane, and nothing inside the artwork may be pure white.

Official game asset, same visual language as the rest of the game: 2D flat vector cartoon, single art direction, uniform thick black outline of constant weight, flat colors with minimal cel-shading, NO gradients, no photorealism, single soft light from the top-left. Locked palette -- #FFD93D sunny yellow, #FF6B35 orange, #FF4D6D pink-red, #4D96FF blue, #6BCB77 green, plus #FFF8E7 cream, #2C2C2C ink black and #FFFFFF white -- use no colors outside that list. Generous safe margin, square canvas, clean readable shapes, mobile-game production quality, cohesive studio look. Simple flat vector game asset, centered, plain white background. No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no cropping.
