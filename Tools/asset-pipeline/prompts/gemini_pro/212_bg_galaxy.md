## Prompt

Picture an entire spiral galaxy that has stopped being astronomy and started being a temple. The huge swirling spiral arms still fill the sky overhead, but now they read as sacred: the arms curve like the vault of a cathedral, colossal ancient stone archways float half-dissolved among the star clusters, shafts of pale holy light fall between the spiral arms, and rings of glowing script orbit slowly in the deep. Keep the palette cold and vast — deep indigo, violet and starlight white with only restrained warm accents — so it stays deep space and never becomes a golden heaven. The mood is: something enormous is being worshipped here, and it is not you yet.

The bottom third of the image must be a clean, empty, walkable plain of compacted stardust where game characters will stand — keep that whole strip completely free of objects, props or clutter. All scenery, structures and landscape belong in the upper two thirds. Do not include any characters, people, or text of any kind. Flat cartoon style with thick black outlines and flat colors matching a vibrant, colorful mobile game — no photorealism, no gradients, no watermark. Square image, full canvas, no cropping.
